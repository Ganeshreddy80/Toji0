## 1. ACCOUNTING SEMANTIC TRACE

A complete source-level trace of existing accounting semantics was performed across:
- `PositionValuationEngine.on_close()` (`research_platform/portfolio_accounting/position_valuation_engine.py`)
- `PortfolioAccountingEngine.apply_fill()` & `apply_close_pnl()` (`research_platform/portfolio_accounting/portfolio_accounting_engine.py`)
- `AccountingService.on_fill()` (`research_platform/portfolio_accounting/accounting_service.py`)
- `TradeLedgerEntry` creation (`research_platform/portfolio_accounting/models.py`)

### Answers to Mandatory Questions:
1. **Is `realized_pnl` gross or net?**  
   The `realized_pnl` stored in `TradeLedgerEntry` (and returned by `on_close()`) is **net of closing leg commission**, but gross of opening leg commission and gross of all slippage:
   $$\text{entry.realized\_pnl} = (\text{exit\_price} - \text{entry\_price}) \cdot \text{quantity} - \text{closing\_commission}$$
2. **Is commission already included?**  
   Closing leg commission is included in `TradeLedgerEntry.realized_pnl`. Opening leg commission is **not** included in `TradeLedgerEntry.realized_pnl`.
3. **Is slippage already included?**  
   **No.** Neither opening nor closing slippage is included in `TradeLedgerEntry.realized_pnl`.
4. **How does normal runtime cash change on BUY?**  
   $$\text{cash}_{\text{after BUY}} = \text{cash}_{\text{before}} - (\text{quantity} \cdot \text{price} + \text{commission} + \text{slippage})$$
5. **How does normal runtime cash change on SELL?**  
   $$\text{cash}_{\text{after SELL}} = \text{cash}_{\text{before}} + (\text{quantity} \cdot \text{price} - \text{commission} - \text{slippage})$$
6. **What exact formula produces authoritative runtime cash?**  
   Total cash generated by history equals initial balance plus all cash received from SELL fills minus all cash spent on BUY fills minus all fees (commissions + slippages) across all fills:
   $$\text{rehydrated\_cash} = \text{initial\_balance} + \text{sell\_cash\_received} - \text{buy\_cash\_spent} - \text{fees}$$

## 2. CORRECT MATHEMATICAL FORMULA

In `AccountingService.rehydrate_from_db()` (`research_platform/portfolio_accounting/accounting_service.py:582-591`):

```python
# 4. Calculate cumulative financial metrics from ledger and positions
realized_pnl = sum(e.realized_pnl for e in db_ledger_entries)
commission = sum(e.commission for e in db_ledger_entries)
slippage = sum(e.slippage for e in db_ledger_entries)
fees = commission + slippage

buy_ledger_spent = sum(e.quantity * e.entry_price for e in db_ledger_entries if e.side == "BUY")
sell_cash_received = sum(e.quantity * e.exit_price for e in db_ledger_entries if e.side == "SELL")
open_outlay = sum(p.quantity * p.entry_price for p in db_positions)

buy_cash_spent = buy_ledger_spent if buy_ledger_spent > 0 else open_outlay
rehydrated_cash = self._initial_balance + sell_cash_received - buy_cash_spent - fees
```

### Mathematical Proof of Correctness:
- For any opening BUY fill: cash decreases by $(\text{quantity} \cdot \text{entry\_price} + \text{commission} + \text{slippage})$.
- For any closing SELL fill: cash increases by $(\text{quantity} \cdot \text{exit\_price} - \text{commission} - \text{slippage})$.
- Net Cash across all fills:
  $$\text{Cash} = B_0 + \sum_{e \in \text{SELL}} (Q_e P_{\text{exit}}) - \sum_{e \in \text{BUY}} (Q_e P_{\text{entry}}) - \sum_{e \in L} (\text{commission}_e + \text{slippage}_e)$$
- If position snapshots exist without historical BUY ledger entries (legacy test isolation mode), `buy_cash_spent` falls back gracefully to `open_outlay`.
