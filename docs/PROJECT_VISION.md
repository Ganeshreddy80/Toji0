## Purpose

This document defines the **vision, mission, and strategic direction** for the Toji platform. It serves as the north star for all product and engineering decisions.

---

## Mission Statement

To build a **production-grade quantitative research platform** that empowers researchers with reproducible analytics, intelligent agents, persistent memory, and seamless tool integration — all within a secure, modular, and scalable architecture.

---

## Vision

Toji envisions a world where quantitative research is:

- **Reproducible** — Every analysis can be replayed, audited, and verified.
- **Intelligent** — AI agents augment human researchers with persistent context and autonomous capabilities.
- **Connected** — Tools, data sources, and workflows interoperate through standardized protocols.
- **Accessible** — Complex research infrastructure is available to teams of all sizes.
